# Postpartum Support 💗

A maternal mental health awareness and support platform developed as part of an ideathon project, focusing on postpartum depression (PPD).

The platform combines an informative webpage, an interactive chatbot, and a wellness quiz to help new mothers understand postpartum mental health, recognize potential symptoms, and access supportive guidance in a welcoming and accessible environment.

## 💡 Problem Statement

Postpartum depression can affect mothers after childbirth, impacting their emotional well-being, daily lives, and ability to navigate early motherhood. However, awareness, understanding, and access to emotional support remain significant challenges.

Many mothers may hesitate to talk about their feelings due to stigma, fear of judgment, or uncertainty about whether their experiences are normal.

## 🎯 Proposed Solution

A web-based platform that provides a safe, approachable starting point for postpartum mental health awareness through:

- Educational content about postpartum depression
- An interactive chatbot for supportive conversations
- A wellness quiz to encourage self-reflection
- An accessible and user-friendly interface
- Awareness of when professional support may be needed

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Informational Homepage
Provides awareness about postpartum depression, its symptoms, and the importance of maternal mental health.

### 🤖 Interactive Chatbot
A chatbot designed to provide empathetic responses, supportive guidance, and information related to postpartum emotional well-being.

### 📝 Wellness Quiz
An interactive quiz that encourages users to reflect on their emotional experiences and learn more about postpartum mental health.

### 💗 Awareness & Support
Promotes open conversations about maternal mental health and encourages mothers to seek support without stigma.

## ⚠️ Disclaimer

This platform is intended for educational and supportive purposes only. The chatbot and wellness quiz are not diagnostic tools and cannot replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Individuals experiencing persistent, severe, or concerning symptoms should seek help from a qualified healthcare professional.
